? FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message below.

Is there a joining fee or a lock-in?

No joining fee and no lock-in. Every plan is month-to-month; you can pause or cancel with 7 days' notice.

Can I try Kinetra before I commit?

Yes. Book a free day pass at any branch through the contact form and a team member will arrange a tour and a trial session.

Can I use any branch with one membership?

Yes. Your membership works at every Kinetra branch, so you can train near home or near work.

What happens after I apply for a membership?

We call you within one working day to confirm your plan, branch and start date. You pay at the branch when you start, and nothing is charged online.

Do you offer diet plans for vegetarians, vegans or Jain diets?

Yes. Nutritionists build plans for vegetarian, vegan, Jain and non-veg diets, using the foods you already eat.

Are the trainers certified?

All coaches hold recognised certifications and are first-aid trained. Apex members get 8 personal training sessions every month.
